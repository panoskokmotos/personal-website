Why I'm writing this

I've spent the last few years building Givelink — a platform that makes in-kind giving specific, trackable, and human. Along the way I read 40+ books a year, competed in HYROX, and thought constantly about what it means to be useful when AI can do almost anything a human can.

This book is my attempt to write down what I've figured out — and to think through the rest in public, with you.
